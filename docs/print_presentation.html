⚠️ Problem Statement

The Chaos of Natural Disasters & Emergency Response Failures

During floods, earthquakes, cyclones, and fires, traditional tools fail citizens when seconds count.

🚫 Lack of Real-Time Shelter Awareness

Victims lack visibility into nearest open shelters, available bed capacity, clean water, or power backup status.

🚧 Hazardous & Blocked Roads

Standard navigation apps guide evacuees into flooded underpasses, collapsed bridges, or standing water hazards.

🏥 Medical Triage Bottlenecks

Emergency rooms overflow while victims cannot locate nearby free ER beds, ICU units, or oxygen reserves.

⌛ Delayed Emergency Triage Guidance

Panicked citizens lack immediate access to localized first-aid protocols and personalized AI triage.

🎯 Project Aim & Objectives

Empowering Humanity with Real-Time Life-Saving Guidance

Our mission is to build a location-aware disaster management ecosystem providing immediate navigation and AI triage.

1. Street-Snapped Safe Navigation

Utilize Open Source Routing Machine (OSRM) to snap driving paths 100% onto actual street roads and bypass flood hazards.

2. Real-Time Medical Triage

Provide live ER/ICU bed metrics and 24/7 direct emergency hotline connections for immediate rescue dispatch.

3. Gemini Predictive Analytics

Harness Google Gemini 1.5 Flash AI to deliver 7-day satellite disaster risk modeling for any global region.

💡 The Proposed Solution

A Unified Emergency Ecosystem Powered by Live Telemetry & AI

AidPulse AI integrates real-world GIS data, live weather metrics, seismic feeds, and Gemini 1.5 Flash AI.

🛣️ OSRM Real Road Engine

Replaces straight-line polylines with 100+ snapped road geometry points following actual streets, highways, and rotaries.

🌧️ Route Weather Telemetry

Compares origin vs. destination weather telemetry (Temperature, Rain mm/h, Wind speed) to warn against flash floods.

⚡ Live USGS Seismic Telemetry

Plots real-time global earthquake feeds with exact Richter magnitude, depth, and official USGS event links.

🌐 Multi-Language Accessibility

Fully localized in 6 major global languages: English, Spanish, Hindi, French, Bengali, and Arabic.

🚀 Tech Stack & Future Vision

Production Architecture & Global Scalability

🛠️ Technical Architecture

  • Frontend: React 18, Vite 8, Leaflet GIS, Lucide Icons.
  • AI Engine: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash REST API (`gemini-1.5-flash`).
  • GIS & Routing: OSRM Engine, OpenStreetMap Overpass & Nominatim.
  • Telemetry: USGS Earthquake Feed & Open-Meteo Weather API.

🌐 Scalability & Future Vision

  • Lightning Speed: Vite production bundle builds in ~130ms.
  • Offline Resilience: Client-side rule engine fallbacks if APIs disconnect.
  • IoT Drone Rescue: Integrating payload dispatch telemetry for disaster zones.
  • Satellite Mesh Sync: Enabling P2P offline mesh emergency sync.
